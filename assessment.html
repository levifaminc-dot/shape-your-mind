Understanding Your PHQ-4 Screening Results

The Patient Health Questionnaire-4 (PHQ-4) is an ultra-brief, validated screening tool consisting of a 2-item anxiety scale (GAD-2) and a 2-item depression scale (PHQ-2). Here is how psychologists interpret cumulative scores:

0 – 2 Points

Normal / Minimal

Suggests healthy emotional balance. Maintain active rest, quality sleep, and regular mindfulness habits.

3 – 5 Points

Mild Psychological Strain

Noticeable tension or brief blues. Recommended to re-evaluate boundaries, exercise, and consider a wellness check-in.

6 – 8 Points

Moderate Symptoms

Symptoms begin to interfere with daily focus and relationships. Consultation with a licensed counselor is strongly advised.

9 – 12 Points

Severe Emotional Distress

Significant psychological burden. Prioritize booking an immediate clinical consultation or contacting our crisis support desk.

Clinical Note: A screening questionnaire cannot replace a professional diagnostic evaluation. If you desire an in-depth clinical diagnosis (e.g. for ADHD, Major Depressive Disorder, or Anxiety Disorders), schedule a Comprehensive Diagnostic Assessment with our clinical psychologists.
