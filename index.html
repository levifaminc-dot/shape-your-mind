Who We Are

Empowering Minds, Restoring Balance & Fostering Resilience

Shape Your Mind Consult (known digitally as ShapeUrMind) was founded on the fundamental belief that psychological well-being is not a luxury—it is the foundation of every thriving individual, family, and organization.

Headquartered in Nigeria with global telehealth reach, our multidisciplinary team brings together psychiatric professionals, occupational therapists, trauma advocates, and counselors to deliver evidence-backed care tailored to African and international cultural realities.

Clinical Rigor

Evidence-based methodologies including CBT, Psychodynamics, and standard psychometrics.

Absolute Confidentiality

Strict clinical ethics and end-to-end encrypted telehealth consultation channels.

Culturally Attuned

Deep empathy for unique socio-cultural realities and workplace demands.

Holistic Continuum

From initial diagnostic evaluation to coping toolkits and ongoing follow-up.
