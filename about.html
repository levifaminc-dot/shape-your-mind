Our Founding Story

Bridging Clinical Excellence with Cultural Empathy

Established as Shape Your Mind Consult (commonly known online as ShapeUrMind), our organization was founded by a collective of psychiatric nurses, occupational therapists, counselors, and mental health advocates.

Too often, emotional pain and psychological distress in our communities are met with silence, superstition, or dismissive attitudes. We founded ShapeUrMind to dismantle this barrier. By combining world-standard clinical interventions (such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy and psychometrics) with deep sensitivity to socio-economic realities, we provide a safe harbor for transformation.

Our Mission

To make compassionate, evidence-based mental healthcare and personal development universally accessible, destigmatized, and actionable for individuals, families, and institutions across Nigeria and beyond.

Our Vision

A society where seeking mental healthcare is embraced as a natural mark of personal strength, emotional resilience is proactively cultivated in every classroom and boardroom, and no one suffers in silence.
